How recommendations work

Fit is a system, not a keyword score.

The engine rejects roles that look impressive but violate a hard constraint. Passing roles are then evaluated for the conditions that determine whether the job is actually sustainable.

Hard gates

Can this job work at all?

Remote geography, live posting, senior scope, product-level AI, and compensation are checked before softer fit signals.

Structural fit

Can someone succeed here?

Team support, technical partners, reporting line, ownership boundaries, operating maturity, and pace matter more than an exciting title.

Evidence fit

Can the candidate prove it?

The résumé is matched to the role’s real work. Tailoring elevates verified evidence; it never manufactures claims or performance numbers.
