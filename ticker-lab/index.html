A personal experiment in making stock research more explainable. Not “what should I buy?” but “why did this company surface, what evidence supports it, and what could make the thesis wrong?”

What it is

A research queue, not a recommendation engine.

Ticker Lab starts with an open-source momentum screener and asks harder questions of it. The original baseline combines trend structure, relative strength, company fundamentals, market conditions, and risk rules. My work is to make those inputs legible, test them honestly, and reshape the experience around better decisions—not more confident predictions.

How the lab works

Three rules before any score earns trust.

01

Explain the signal.

Every surfaced company should show the evidence behind its ranking—not hide the decision inside one polished number.

02

Test before trusting.

Strategy claims need reproducible tests, realistic timing, honest benchmarks, and periods the model didn’t learn from.

03

Make uncertainty visible.

Stale data, missing fields, assumptions, and competing evidence belong in the interface—not buried in fine print.

The important boundary

Research prompts, not financial advice.

Ticker Lab is an experiment in research quality and decision design. A company appearing in the lab is a reason to investigate—not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything.
