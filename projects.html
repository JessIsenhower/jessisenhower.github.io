About Coherence

A fictional company used as a real design sandbox.

Coherence began as an interactive-fiction idea. I use it to design and test real GTM systems without pretending a fictional company has fictional customer results. Working tools are labeled as working. Synthetic data stays synthetic. Blueprints are not described as deployments.
