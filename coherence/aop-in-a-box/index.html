AOP in a Box

The problem is not building ten planning models. The problem is making sure they agree. A growth target that ignores ramp, a pipeline plan that ignores cycle time, or a comp plan that rewards the wrong motion will eventually reconcile itself in missed numbers.

The operating question

Can the plan survive contact with its own assumptions?

I built AOP in a Box to make the dependencies visible before the year starts. Each model hands a defined output to the next. When one assumption changes, the downstream consequence should be inspectable.

DecisionInput that mattersWhat breaks when it is wrong
Bookings targetStarting ARR, retention, growth target, timingThe entire plan inherits an impossible top line.
Pipeline requirementWin rate, ASP, sales cycle, source mixCoverage looks healthy while timing or conversion makes the target unreachable.
CapacityProductivity, ramp, attrition, hiring datesQuota is assigned to capacity that does not exist yet.
Territory + quotaMarket potential, rep capacity, account distributionThe plan bakes inequity and coverage gaps into execution.
CompensationCompany priorities, role behavior, quota designPeople are paid to optimize against the operating plan.
ForecastActual execution versus plan assumptionsVariance is reported after the fact instead of diagnosed.

Data
