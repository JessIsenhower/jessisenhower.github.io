AOP in a Box

A connected annual-planning system that makes the dependencies among growth targets, pipeline, capacity, quotas, territories, marketing, compensation, forecasting, and executive decisions visible before the plan becomes a spreadsheet negotiation.

The planning problem

Annual plans often fail at the handoffs.

Each function can produce a reasonable model while the company still produces an incoherent plan. AOP in a Box treats planning as one connected system of assumptions, decisions, owners, and operating consequences.

Targets outrun capacity

Finance approves growth before hiring, ramp, productivity, and attrition are reconciled.

Pipeline does not balance

Coverage and source targets do not reflect conversion, cycle timing, or segment economics.

Incentives conflict

Territories, quotas, and compensation reward behavior that does not match the operating plan.

The shared planning spine

Every decision hands an approved output to the next.

Built artifacts

The public preview is backed by working models.

The portfolio distinguishes what is operational from what is instructional. Spreadsheet models are editable planning companions. Agent pages are build blueprints unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

Build blueprints

Thirty-four GTM agents

Workflow, tooling, implementation steps, cost, controls, and impact—not claims of production deployment.

AI support

Improve inspection without hiding the model.

AI should make assumptions, conflicts, and consequences easier to understand. Material planning decisions remain visible and accountable to leaders.

Useful applications

  • Detect inconsistent assumptions across models
  • Recalculate scenarios and narrate business consequences
  • Identify missing owners, dates, and dependencies
  • Inspect pipeline, capacity, quota, and forecast risk
  • Prepare executive decision briefs and meeting agendas
  • Maintain an assumption and decision log
