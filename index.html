Point of view

AI is useful when it removes friction, exposes better evidence, or lets people make a better decision faster. If it only adds another layer of output, it has probably made the system worse.

How I tend to work

Find the break. Make it visible. Fix the system around it.

I usually enter through an operating problem: a forecast nobody trusts, a CPQ process nobody can explain, dashboards that report but do not guide action, a merger that left three versions of the truth, or an AI workflow that looks clever until it meets real governance.

I use data, systems, process design, facilitation, and increasingly AI to get from that mess to something people can actually run.
