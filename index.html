My point of view

AI fluency isn’t transformation. The advantage comes from redesigning the work around it.

How I work

Start with reality.
Design for use.

I’m most valuable where the problem crosses business, technical, and user perspectives.

01

Clarify the outcome.

Define what should become clearer, faster, or more useful—and why it matters.

02

Build an honest picture.

Compare what people say with what the data shows. Map the work, decisions, and handoffs. Watch how it actually gets done.

03

Design across the system.

Balance the business outcome, the experience of the people doing the work, and what can realistically be sustained.

04

Make adoption part of the design.

Create clarity, useful governance, and room to adapt when reality contradicts the plan.
