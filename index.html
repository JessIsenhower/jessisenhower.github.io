01 · The story

I’ve spent my career translating between worlds.

Not because translation sounds elegant. Because most business problems live in the gaps—between what leaders think is happening and what teams experience every day.

01

I started closest to the customer.

Enterprise sales and customer success taught me to listen for the real problem underneath the stated one. At Webex and Cisco, that instinct became structured customer-success onboarding and more than 100 in-person CSM trainings.

02

Then I built the systems around what I heard.

At TIBCO, I implemented Gainsight, designed health scoring and risk architecture, and helped teams see customer reality sooner. That work led naturally into enablement, leadership development, and end-to-end revenue operations.

03

Now I work at the seams.

Sales, Customer Success, Finance, Product. Salesforce, Gong, CPQ, quote-to-cash. I connect the operating system so people can make better decisions—and use AI where it creates leverage without losing the humans it’s meant to help.

02 · My philosophy
“The goal is never the dashboard. The goal is the decision the dashboard makes possible.”
Signal over noise

Start with the decision.

Before adding a field, report, or process, get clear on what someone needs to understand or do differently.

Foundation before flourish

Make AI useful, not ornamental.

AI compounds what already exists. Clean foundations, trusted data, and clear ownership turn possibility into adoption.

Confidence creates movement

Design for how people learn.

When people understand the why, confidence follows. When confidence grows, performance changes.

Systems are human

Build with the field, not at it.

The best operating models reflect the reality of the people using them. Trust is part of the architecture.
