About

I like figuring out how things really work.

That is probably the cleanest explanation for both my career and the things I do when nobody assigned me a project.

I’ve spent most of my career somewhere between operations, enablement, data, systems, and the people expected to use them. I like the part where the neat process diagram meets reality and starts misbehaving.

I read a lot, especially strategy, psychology, systems, science fiction, and anything that gives me a new lens on a problem. I also build with AI for work and for fun. Some experiments become useful tools. Some become cautionary tales. Both are worth keeping.

Coherence is the strangest example. It started as an interactive-fiction idea. Then I wanted to know how its fictional company would actually operate, so I built the annual plan, sales system, assessments, spreadsheets, and agent blueprints. The company is fictional. The operating work is real design work.

Outside of that: I live on the Texas coast, I fish, I read, and I am entirely capable of turning a casual question into a small research project.
