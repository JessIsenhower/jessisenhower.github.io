Revenue intelligence · public reconstruction

Turn a pile of GTM signals into a short list of decisions.

This case study reconstructs a weekly revenue-insight workflow I built in RevOps. The operating pattern is real. The companies, deal names, values, and screenshots below are synthetic so the reasoning can be inspected without exposing production data.

The problem

Leaders already have dashboards. They still have to figure out what changed and what deserves attention.

CRM reports show one slice. Conversation intelligence shows another. Slack, BI, enrichment, and forecast calls add more. The failure mode is not lack of information. It is forcing leaders to reconcile all of it manually before they can decide where to intervene.

Source 01

CRM

Stage, amount, close-date movement, owner, next step, forecast category.

Source 02

Conversations

Economic-buyer access, objections, competitor mentions, next-step language, inactivity.

Source 03

Performance data

Win rate, cycle, conversion, attainment, segment baselines, historical patterns.

Source 04

Operating signals

Pricing exceptions, support issues, marketing activity, leadership notes, enrichment.

Decision architecture

Do not ask the model to “find insights.” Define what earns attention first.

The useful design work is upstream of the language model: signal definitions, thresholds, source priority, evidence requirements, and who owns the response.

Examples of explicit rules

  • Flag material close-date movement only when value and timing make it forecast-relevant.
  • Separate missing economic-buyer evidence from general engagement.
  • Compare rep behavior against segment baselines instead of one global average.
  • Require source evidence for every recommended intervention.
  • Do not let the model change CRM, forecast, or coaching status on its own.
Close-date slip30+ days on material opportunityInspect compelling event, procurement path, and seller forecast call.
Champion riskHigh engagement, no access to powerTest whether contact is a product champion or a commercial champion.
Conversation silenceLate-stage deal with no customer meeting in 14 daysInspect next-step ownership and whether the deal should remain in stage.
Rep patternConversion or activity changes outside normal rangeGive the manager a coaching question, not an automated performance verdict.

What changed

Forecast

Two enterprise opportunities moved into the following month, reducing current-period commit by $310K.

Qualification

One of the slipped deals has strong product engagement but no confirmed economic-buyer meeting or funding path.

Execution

Mid-market stage-two conversion improved for the second week while average time in stage fell.

Evidence: Salesforce close-date history + forecast category changes · Gong stakeholder and next-step signals · segment conversion baseline.

Where leadership should intervene

  1. Enterprise Deal A: Ask the seller to test the internal sponsor against the commercial-champion standard before restoring the prior close date.
  2. Enterprise Deal B: Review procurement timing and dated compelling event with the manager; the CRM next step is seller-owned and stale.
  3. Mid-market team: Inspect what changed in discovery behavior before treating the conversion improvement as a durable trend.

What not to conclude yet

The mid-market improvement is two weeks old and does not yet justify changing targets, capacity assumptions, or coaching standards. The brief surfaces the pattern; leadership decides whether it is signal or noise.

Human + AI boundary

AI compresses the inspection work. It does not own the judgment.

Operator owns

The system of truth

Metric definitions, thresholds, source hierarchy, stage rules, segment baselines, data access, exception logic, and who is accountable for acting.

AI accelerates

The synthesis layer

Grouping evidence, comparing changes, drafting the narrative, proposing questions, and turning many records into a reviewable first pass.

Human review

The decision

Whether the evidence is sufficient, whether context is missing, what action is appropriate, and whether anything should change in forecast or coaching.

Guardrail

Trace every claim

Material recommendations should point back to a source record or defined rule. A polished sentence without inspectable evidence does not qualify as an insight.
